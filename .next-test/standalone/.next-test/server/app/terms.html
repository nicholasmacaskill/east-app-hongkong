8. Code of Conduct

In joining EAST High Performance Centre and the EAST App, you are expected to adhere to the following Code of Conduct. Failure to adhere to this Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action, including but not limited to suspension or loss of privileges related to Gym facilities, activities, and events. No refunds will be issued in the event of such disciplinary action.

General Behaviour

  • Always clean up after yourself and return equipment (weights, mats, etc.) to their designated areas.
  • Keep the facility tidy; dispose of trash properly and respect communal spaces.

Respect for Others

  • Treat all members, staff, coaches and other athletes with respect and courtesy at all times.
  • Avoid disruptive behaviour, including excessive noise or distractions during classes.

Clean Up After Yourself

  • Wipe down equipment after use.
  • Return weights, mats, and other equipment to their designated places.

Respect Personal Space

  • Maintain a safe distance from others while exercising.
  • Avoid crowding around someone else’s workout area.

Refrain from Excessive Noise

  • Keep conversations to a reasonable volume.
  • Avoid loud grunting or shouting during workouts.

Limit Time on Equipment

  • Be mindful of peak hours and limit your time on machines if others are waiting.
  • Use the “5-minute rule” to let others know you’ll be done soon.

Use Headphones for Music

  • If listening to music, keep the volume at a level that doesn’t disturb others.

Follow Gym Guidelines

  • Adhere to any posted rules or guidelines regarding facility usage.
  • Respect any reservations or time constraints for classes or specific areas.

Ask Before Joining a Group

  • If a group is using equipment, ask politely before joining in or using shared resources.

Don’t Interrupt Others

  • Avoid interrupting someone’s workout for social conversations or asking to work in unless necessary.

Dress Appropriately

  • Wear appropriate workout attire and footwear.
  • Avoid clothing with offensive graphics or slogans.

Be Mindful of Personal Belongings

  • Keep your belongings in designated areas or lockers, not on the gym floor.
  • Do not leave personal items unattended.

Encourage Others

  • Be supportive of other members’ efforts and achievements.
  • Avoid negative or judgmental comments.
  • Be courteous to gym staff; listen to their instructions.
  • Treat all other members with respect and kindness.

Report Issues

  • Notify staff about broken or malfunctioning equipment.
  • Report unsafe behavior or conditions.

Safety and Compliance

  • Follow all posted guidelines and instructions from staff regarding safety and equipment usage. Adjust weight and settings to ensure safe usage. Use equipment for its intended purpose.
  • Report any unsafe conditions, injuries, or accidents immediately.

Surveillance Notice

Please be aware that CCTV cameras are in effect 24/7 for safety and security purposes. Any inappropriate behavior will be addressed accordingly, with disciplinary actions ranging from warnings to termination of Membership.

Zero Tolerance Policies

  • There is zero tolerance for harassment, bullying, derogatory remarks, or any form of discrimination.
  • Underage drinking, tobacco, vaping, and gambling are strictly prohibited.

Social Media and Public Behaviour

Maintain a positive representation of the Gym on social media platforms. Any negative or defamatory posts about the Gym or its members will be addressed appropriately.
